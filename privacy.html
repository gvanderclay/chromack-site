Chromack Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026

Chromack does not collect, store or share any personal data.

The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party code, and it does not connect to the internet. Your progress (levels solved, your moves, your stickers and your settings) is saved only on your device, inside the app. Deleting the app deletes it.

Because we receive no data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on your behalf.

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the app changes.
