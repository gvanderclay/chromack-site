Chromack

A calm colour puzzle. Swap tiles back into a smooth gradient, and collect a painted sticker for every solve.

Support

Something not working, or an idea? Email gvanderclay@gmail.com.

How to play

Drag a tile onto another to swap them, or tap one tile and then another. Tiles with a cream corner are fixed in place. When every colour flows into the next, the puzzle is solved.
